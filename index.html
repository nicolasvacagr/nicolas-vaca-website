ABOUT A LOT ↓
Curiosity has always been my default setting. I spent years chasing a tennis ball across courts around the world, learning that the best version of yourself is built through repetition. When I hung up the racket, I channelled that same obsession into something else: ideas. Today I spend my time going down rabbit holes most people disregard. Here is where I expose my interest in rabbit holes to the world.

As someone who since early on found pleasure in knowing more than was necessary about things only few found interesting I still spend my free time learning about fun facts and unknown stories.

Right now, I'm specially interested on artificial intelligence, evolutionary psychology, relationship dynamics, and personal development. But one of my core beliefs is that anything can be interesting if you ask the right question and go deep enough into it.

Based and work in Madrid 🇪🇸

Untold — An Introduction
What Untold is, and why I started it!

Ralph Waldo Emerson once said that in the minds of geniuses, we find our own neglected thoughts. The more I learn, the more I find this to be true. Untold is where I and you go to explore those neglected thoughts, by listening to people far smarter than me.

I look to speak with researchers, explorers, thinkers, and anyone with an idea worth spreading, a story worth telling, or a question more of us should be asking ourselves. Join me as I feed my curiosity, one conversation at a time.
