Nicolas Vaca
Tennis Player. Curious Mind. Podcast Host.

Born in Bogotá. Built in Madrid. Driven by curiosity.

Who I am
A few honest lines.

I grew up in Bogotá, Colombia, the youngest of five in a loud, loving family. Sport taught me discipline before school did — years on the tennis court shaped how I think about effort, pressure, and what it means to keep going.

A move to Mallorca, then Madrid, then a degree in Economics at IE University pulled me toward business and ideas. An internship at AWS turned tech into a career path I hadn't expected. Today I work in the startup ecosystem at Amazon, and host Untold — a podcast about ideas, people, and the questions worth asking out loud.

This page is a record of how I got here.

From the beginning
The story so far
2003
Born in Bogotá, Colombia
Born on the evening of June 14th, Nicolas is the youngest in a big family of five children. His parents, Viviana and Oscar, raised him in Bogotá surrounded by siblings, sport, and a strong sense of family.
2009
Colegio San Carlos, Bogotá
Starts school at Colegio San Carlos in Bogotá, where he would spend the next eleven years. Raised in a catholic institution, Nicolas learned early the value of discipline, hard work, and community.
2012–2019
The tennis years
During his late childhood and most of his teens, Nicolas dedicated himself to becoming a better tennis player in his home city of Bogotá. The court was where he spent most of his time — competing, training, and learning what it means to be relentless.
2020–2021
Rafa Nadal Academy, Manacor
Nicolas moves to the small town of Manacor on the Mediterranean island of Mallorca to finish high school and double down on his dream of becoming a professional tennis player at the Rafa Nadal Academy.
2021
Economics degree, IE University — Madrid
Decides to stay in Spain to complete his undergraduate studies. He moves to Madrid and begins his Economics degree at IE University — a new chapter, a new city, and a new direction.
2023
First race — Half Marathon
Completes his first ever race: a half marathon, finishing in 1:38:49. The first of many finish lines.
2023
First job — SP Developments, Miami
Works his first job with Miami-based real estate development company SP Developments, supporting the CEO in daily tasks. His first taste of how business is actually run.
2024
Copenhagen Marathon — on a whim
Spontaneously runs the Copenhagen Marathon during a weekend visit to friends in Denmark — with essentially no prior training. Classic.
2024
AWS Internship
Spends the summer interning at Amazon Web Services. The corporate and Big Tech world had an unexpected impact on his career direction — making tech a serious option where wealth management and real estate had previously dominated.
2025
Hyrox Doubles, Málaga
Competes in a Hyrox doubles race with his best friend and brother Juan in Málaga, Spain. They cross the finish line in 1:01:52.
2025
Economics graduate — Dean's List · AWS full time
Graduates from his Economics degree as part of the Dean's List — the top of his class. Transitions directly into a full-time role at Amazon Web Services.
2026
Untold — the podcast begins
Starts Untold and realises the things we want from life no one will give us. We need to go for them.
