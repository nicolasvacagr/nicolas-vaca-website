Untold — An Introduction
What is Untold, and why did I start it?

Ralph Waldo Emerson once said that in the minds of geniuses, we find our own neglected thoughts. The more I learn, the more I find this to be true. Untold is where I and you go to explore those neglected thoughts, by listening to people far smarter than me.

I look to speak with researchers, explorers, thinkers, and anyone with an idea worth spreading, a story worth telling, or a question more of us should be asking ourselves. Join me as I feed my curiosity, one conversation at a time.

About the Show
Driving Untold

André Gide once wrote: "Everything that needs to be said has already been said. But since no one was listening, everything must be said again." That is what drives this show.

I started it because I kept coming across ideas that deserved a bigger audience — research buried in papers, questions people should ask themselves but aren't, stories that never made it to the mainstream.

The format is simple: long-form, unscripted. No agenda. Just following the conversation wherever it goes.
