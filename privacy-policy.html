Overview

Plain-language summary: This site is a personal project — a podcast, newsletter, and portfolio run by Nicolas Vaca. I collect only what I need to operate the contact form and newsletter, I do not sell your data, and I do not run advertising. Full details are below.

This Privacy Policy explains how Nicolas Vaca ("I", "me", or "my") collects, uses, and protects personal information when you visit nicolasvacagr.com (the "Site") and interact with its features, including the contact form, newsletter sign-up, and embedded third-party content.

By using this Site you agree to the practices described in this Policy. If you disagree, please do not submit any personal information through the Site.

Data We Collect

I collect information in two ways: information you provide directly, and information collected automatically when you visit the Site.

Information you provide directly:

  • Contact form: your name, email address, and the content of your message.
  • Newsletter sign-up: your email address.

Information collected automatically:

  • Basic server-level logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited, referring URL, timestamp) — retained briefly for security and diagnostic purposes.
  • If an analytics tool is active on the Site, aggregate, anonymised usage statistics (pages viewed, session duration). No individual profiles are built.

I do not collect payment information, government IDs, or sensitive personal data of any kind.

Contact Form

When you submit the contact form, the information you enter (name, email, message) is sent directly to me so I can respond to your enquiry. This data is:

  • Used solely to reply to your message.
  • Not added to any marketing list without your separate, explicit consent.
  • Retained only as long as necessary to handle your enquiry and any reasonable follow-up period (typically up to 12 months), after which it is deleted.

The legal basis for processing this data is legitimate interest (responding to a message you chose to send me) and, where applicable, your consent.

Newsletter

If you subscribe to the newsletter through this Site, your email address is passed to Substack, the third-party platform I use to write and distribute it. Once your email is submitted, it is governed by Substack's Privacy Policy.

By subscribing you consent to:

  • Receiving periodic emails about ideas, articles, podcast episodes, and related content I publish.
  • Your email address being stored and processed by Substack on my behalf.

You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link included in every email, or by emailing me directly. Unsubscribing removes you from future sends; it does not automatically delete historical data held by Substack — for that, please refer to their deletion request process.

Cookies & Tracking

This Site itself sets minimal or no first-party cookies. However, embedded third-party players (e.g. YouTube, Spotify) may set their own cookies when you interact with them. These are governed by the respective third parties' cookie policies.

If I add analytics in the future, I will update this section and, where required by law, request your consent before setting non-essential cookies.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain embedded features but will not prevent you from reading content on this Site.

Your Rights

Depending on your country of residence, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: request a copy of the personal data I hold about you.
  • Correction: ask me to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: request that I delete your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restriction: ask me to restrict processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent: where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time without affecting prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact me at the address in the Contact section below. I will respond within 30 days. For data held by Substack, YouTube, or other third parties, requests must be directed to them directly.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. As the Site is operated from Spain, the relevant authority is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD) — aepd.es →

Data Retention

  • Contact form submissions: retained for up to 12 months from the date of your last communication, then deleted.
  • Newsletter subscribers: retained by Substack for as long as you are subscribed. After unsubscribing, Substack's own retention schedule applies.
  • Server logs: typically retained for 30–90 days for security purposes, then deleted automatically.

I do not retain personal data longer than necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.

Security

I take reasonable precautions to protect the information you share with me, including using HTTPS encryption for all data in transit. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. I cannot guarantee absolute security and encourage you not to share sensitive personal information through the contact form.

Minors

This Site is not directed at children under the age of 16. I do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a minor has submitted information through this Site, please contact me and I will delete it promptly.
